About

ChatGPT Tips Watch collects practical tips about ChatGPT and OpenAI tooling (Codex, ChatGPT features and dots, custom instructions, plugins, model choice, prompting and the OpenAI API) from a fixed list of builders on X: @OpenAI, @ChatGPT, @OpenAIDevs, @thsottiaux, @pvncher, @dkundel, @romainhuet, @reach_vb, @TheRealAdamG, @steipete, @gabrielchua, @kagigz, @_samirism, @kevins8, @danshipper, @simonw.

Only ChatGPT and OpenAI tooling is covered here. Tips about Claude or other AI tools are left out, even when they come from the same accounts; a tip about a tool that works with several agents is included only when it names ChatGPT, Codex or an OpenAI product.

How it works

  • A daily run reads these accounts' recent public posts (plus threads, quoted posts and linked videos), keeps only practical ChatGPT and OpenAI-tooling content, and merges new tips into data/tips.json, deduplicated by source.
  • Tips are paraphrased, not quoted verbatim, and every one links to its exact source post, article or video (with a timestamp when it comes from a video). When an account shares someone else's post, the original author is credited.
  • Nothing is invented: no numbers or claims appear that aren't in the source. Video summaries are added only after a video has been watched; tips marked "from transcript" were written from auto-captions only.
  • Each topic is split into subcategories that come from the content. Tips from the same post, article or video are grouped into one card; when a source's tips span subcategories, the card sits in the subcategory holding most of its tips and the others get a "See also" link.
  • Every item belongs to an edition: the date (Mountain Time) of the daily run that first added it. The top of the home page shows the latest edition; use the edition menu to view any single edition.
  • Off-topic posts are skipped.
